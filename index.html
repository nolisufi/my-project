01 — Origins

From Peja to Every Kosovar Table

Founded on November 19, 2003 in Peja, Kosovo, Vita Milk began as the country's first dairy to adopt UHT (Ultra-High Temperature) processing. Backed by Devolli Group and a partnership with Tetra Pak, Vita brought world-class dairy technology to Kosovo.

Where Quality Begins

Vita's state-of-the-art facility in Peja, Kosovo is built on a foundation of modern European dairy technology. Every liter of milk passes through rigorous quality control — from farm collection to UHT processing to the iconic Tetra Pak carton.
