Our Story

Founded in 1940 in Hong Kong, Vitasoy began with a simple mission: to bring affordable, nutritious soy milk to the people.

Over 86 years, we've grown from a single soy milk producer into a global plant-based food & beverage leader — distributed across 40+ markets worldwide.
