Our Story

From Peja to Every Kosovar Home

From Peja to Every Kosovar Table

Founded on November 19, 2003 in Peja, Kosovo, Vita Milk began as the country's first dairy to adopt UHT (Ultra-High Temperature) processing. Backed by Devolli Group and a partnership with Tetra Pak, Vita brought world-class dairy technology to Kosovo — making fresh, long-life milk accessible to every family.

For over 20 years, Vita has grown from a single production line into Kosovo's most trusted dairy brand. Our tagline — "Me ty u rrita" (I grew up with you) — reflects a generation of Kosovars who have grown up with Vita on their breakfast table.

November 19, 2003

Vita Milk founded in Peja, Kosovo — the country's first UHT dairy facility

2003

Strategic partnership established with Tetra Pak for aseptic packaging technology

2005–2015

Expanded product line to include yogurt, ayran, kos, and creams — becoming a full-range dairy

2023

Celebrated 20th anniversary with the campaign "Me ty u rrita" — 20 years of nourishing Kosovar families

Today

A household name across Kosovo, trusted by families, restaurants, and bakeries nationwide
